# Privacy Policy — Home Media Catalog

Last updated: [date]

Home Media Catalog ("the app") is developed by Albaintor inc, France
("we"). Contact: albaintor@github.com.

## Summary
The app works locally on your device. We operate no account system,
no analytics and no advertising, and we do not receive your
library, ratings or searches. Data leaves your device only to reach
services that you configure yourself.

## Data stored on your device
Your media catalogue, watch status, personal ratings and settings are
stored locally on your iPhone. API keys and passwords are stored in the
iOS Keychain, are restricted to this device, and are not synced through
iCloud.

## Services you connect yourself
Depending on what you configure, the app talks directly to:
- your own media server (Home Media Catalog server, Kodi, SMB, SFTP,
  FTP, NFS) on your network or over the Internet;
- cloud storage you sign in to (e.g. OneDrive, iCloud Drive);
- The Movie Database (TMDB), using your own TMDB key, to fetch public
  metadata, artwork and streaming availability. TMDB receives the
  titles and identifiers that are looked up, and your IP address.
These services are governed by their own privacy policies. We have no
access to the data exchanged with them.

## Optional AI recommendations
The Streaming tab offers optional AI recommendations. They are off
until you (1) enter your own API key for OpenAI, Anthropic or Google
and (2) turn on the consent toggle for that provider.

When you run an AI search, the app sends the following directly from
your device to the provider you selected, under your own account:
- the text of your search (and your previous search, to refine it);
- up to about 35 titles you have rated, with your personal rating,
  genres and a short synopsis;
- public TMDB candidate titles, your region and the app language.
Titles you watched but did not rate are used only on your device, to
exclude them from results.

Purpose: to generate recommendations you asked for. Legal basis: your
consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). The provider may retain requests under its
own terms (for example, for abuse monitoring), and may process them
outside the EU, including in the United States. Google may use content
sent with free-tier Gemini API keys to improve its products; refer to
the terms of your chosen provider:
- OpenAI: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
- Anthropic: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy
- Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Withdrawing consent: turn off the toggle or delete the key in Settings.
No further data is sent. Data already sent stays subject to the
provider's terms.

Optional key synchronization: if you choose to, the app can send your
AI key over an encrypted connection to your own paired Home Media
Catalog server. We never receive it.

## Retention
We hold no data about you. Local data stays until you delete it or
uninstall the app.

## Your rights
Under the GDPR, you can access, correct or delete your data and
withdraw your consent at any time. Because the data stays on your
device, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the app. For
data held by a third-party service, contact that service. You can lodge
a complaint with the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 4.

## Changes
We will update this page and its date when our practices change.
